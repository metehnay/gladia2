Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

PlantWho ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Oguz Wst. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the PlantWho mobile application.

Information We Collect

Camera & Photo Library Access: We request camera and photo library access solely to photograph plants for identification and diagnosis. Photos are processed through our secure API proxy and sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis. Photos are processed in real-time and are not stored on any server.

Plant Data: Your garden data (saved plants, reminders, preferences) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this data.

AI Chat: Messages sent to the AI Plant Expert are processed by Google Gemini API through our secure proxy. Chat history is stored locally on your device only.

Device Identifier: We collect an anonymous device identifier solely for the purpose of enforcing daily usage limits. This identifier cannot be used to personally identify you.

Push Notifications: If you enable reminders, we use local push notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Browsing history
  • Payment information (handled entirely by Apple App Store / Google Play)

How We Use Your Information

  • To identify plants and diagnose plant health issues via AI
  • To provide personalized plant care recommendations
  • To send local reminder notifications you configure
  • To enforce fair usage limits on free tier
  • To improve app functionality

Data Storage and Security

All user data (plants, reminders, chat history, preferences) is stored locally on your device. We operate a secure API proxy (Cloudflare Workers) that forwards requests to Google Gemini. This proxy does not log or store any user content.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini AI: Used for plant identification, diagnosis, and expert chat. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Used as a secure API proxy. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Used for subscription payments. Subject to their respective privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat: Used for subscription management. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

PlantWho offers optional paid subscriptions (Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime). Payment is processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

PlantWho is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the application
  • You can manage notification permissions in your device settings
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your App Store or Google Play account
  • EU/EEA users have additional rights under GDPR including data access, rectification, and erasure

Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, data retention is entirely under your control. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
